Program registration

Q. I was registered in the Social Day-cruising program last season. Do I need to register again for the current season, or can I simply update my availability? ​

A. The program database is erased at the end of the season. So, you should register afresh each season.

Q. I have registered my boat for one or more events, but I chose not to open an account. What do I need to know about this? ​

A. You can participate without opening an account. However, this does have some drawbacks.

We don't have your email address, so you won't receive communications about event cancellation or crew background.

We don't know your crewing requirements. So, you may be assigned inexperienced crew.

We don't know your lower and upper occupancy limits, so you will be assigned a single crew member.

In case you subsequently decide to open an account, simply go to the program web page and follow the links to open a boat owner account. Make sure to spell your boat name exactly as you did when you originally registered. Please refer to the confirmation email you received at that time. ​

Q. I have registered as crew for one or more events, but I chose not to open an account. What do I need to know about this? ​

A. Not openng an account is a valid choice. However, there are some drawbacks.

First of all, we don't have your email address, so if one of the events for which you are registered were to be cancelled, you would not receive notification.

Then, even if you are an NSC member, we don't have your membership number, so in the event of over-subscription, you will be low on the priority list.

Finally, we know nothing about your sailing background, so you will likely be paired with crew-mates who have no or little sailing experience.

In case you subsequently decide to open an account, simply go to the program web page and follow the links to open a crew account. Make sure to spell your name exactly as you did when you originally registered.d. Please refer to the confirmation email you received at that time. ​

Q. I want to update my personal details.  What should I do? ​

A. Repeat the registration procedure, using your revised personal details.  This resets your availability for ALL future events to “I am not available”.  Therefore, for those events for which you are still available, change your availability to “I am available”. ​

Q. I made a typing error in my last name when registering.  What should I do? ​

A. You must enter your first and last name to register initially, and to make subsequent updates.  Both names must be spelt identically on both occasions.  If you use a spelling that is different from the one you used initially, a new account will be created. ​

If you used an incorrect spelling initially, you can create a new account with the correct spelling.  However, the original account will continue to exist, so update the availability informatioin for this account to "I am not available". ​

Event regstration

Q. I have registered for one or more events.  But, now I find I can’t participate in one of them.  What should I do?

A. On the program website cancel your availability for the relevant event. ​

Q. I did not register for a particular event.  Now I find I can participate.  What should I do?

A. On the program website register your availability for the relevant event. ​

Q. I registered for an event, but then I was unable to attend. However, I did not cancel my registration. What happens? ​

A. If you are not present at the posted meeting time, the space reserved for you may be assigned to and standby or it may go unused. In either case, you will be given low-priority in future events. ​

Q. I live some distance from the club, and the weather looks questionable.  How will I find out if the event is going ahead? ​

A. On the morning of the event, the event coordinator will be in touch with the event’s boat owners.  They will decide which boats (if any) will sail.  Crew members will be informed by email as soon as a decision has been made. ​

Q. I registered for some events, but my name does not appear on the event calendar.  What should I do? ​

A. For technical reasons, updating the calendar involves action by the program coordinator.  Therefore, there can be significant delay before updates appear. ​

If the calendat still does not reflect your availability, check that the email you received when you registered does not contain errors.

Q. I cancelled my registration for an event, but my name still appears on the calendar entry for that date. Why is this?

A. If the calendar does not reflect your availability, check that the email you received when you updated your availability does not contain errors.

The most common explanation is that you used a spelling of your name that differs from the spelling you used when you originally registered.

Another possibility is that you cancelled your registration for a different date.

Q. I am available for a particular event, but I can’t get there by 12:45, or I must leave before 5:00.  Can I still participate? ​

A. Unfortunately, and in fairness to your fellow crew members, you have to miss this event.   ​

Assignments

Q. When I consult the calendar in the days leading up to an event, I find that I am assigned to a different boat each time. Why is this?? ​

A. Every time someone registers or changes their availability, the neural network recalculates the crew assignments in the search for the most compliant solution. This results in crew being moved between boats. ​

Q. My partner and I would like to crew on the same boat. Can this be arranged? ​

A. The principal aim of the program is to support the community of cruising sailors. If you are looking for a recreational activity for you and your partner to share, we suggest you look into some of the other programs offered by the club. ​

Q. My name regularly appears on the wait-list. Why is thia? ​

A. NSC members are given priority over non-members. In order to be recognised as a member, you must enter a valid NSC membership number when registering. Otherwise, you will be treated as a non-member. ​

If you have been a no-show, your priority will be downgraded, whether or not you are a member. ​

If you register within seven days of an event, spaces already assigned (whether to members or non-members) may not be available to you. ​

Q. I am wait-listed for an event. How will I know if a place becomes available? ​

A. In case a crew member withdraws, a place may become available.  Then the calendar will be updated.  However, the calndar is frozen at 10:00 AM on the day of the event.  Wait list entries are in priority order. ​

Q. I have registered, but my name no longer appears on the calendar. Why is this? ​

A. If you have been a no-show on more than one occasion, your account will be deleted. If you later choose to participate, you must open a new account. ​

Getting involved

Q. What are the duties of the event coordinator?

A. ​

  • Have a copy of the crew assignments for the event.
  • Arrive no later than 12:40.
  • Greet arriving owners and crew.  Answer any questions they may have.
  • Ensure all crew get connected with the owner of the boat to which they are assigned. Sometimes crew (and very rarely owners) fail to show up.  In such cases, it may be necessary to reassign crew in order to meet the crewing needs of the boat owners. Despite this, it is important to maintain an embarkation time of 1:00 PM.
  • If sailing is in doubt due to the existing or forecast weather conditions, the program coordinator can take over.  In our experience, this happens roughly once per season.
